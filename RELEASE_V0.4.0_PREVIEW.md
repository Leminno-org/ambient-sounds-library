# v0.4.0-preview — original procedural ambient sound experiments

This engineering **pre-release** contains **41 independent original
procedurally synthesized PCM16 WAV files**, each 24 seconds, stereo, 48 kHz,
roughly 4.61 MB. There are no third-party recordings or music in this new
collection.

Each environment name describes an **artificial sound-design impression**:
synthetic waves, simulated air movement, modeled impacts, resonances, noise,
insect-like trills or animal-like calls. No recording of a real owl, frog,
forest, train or city is represented as original. The audio is dedicated to
the public domain under CC0 1.0; the code, seed algorithm, exact WAV SHA-256
and measurements are recorded in `staged-originals.json`.

This release extends the **sound content candidates** for the planned Android
catalog from 22 existing items to 63 combined candidates (17 separately
credited CC0 recordings + 5 in-app mathematical noise generators + these
41 unique WAVs). It backs all 24 planned starter IDs, 60 beta IDs and all
15 scene recipe source sets **at the content stage**. App integration,
auditory realism, loop listening, content label checks, and human approval
are **not yet complete**. The 120-entry full catalog remains a plan.

The seven old recorded CC0 previews remain at their previously published
immutable `v0.1.0-preview`/`v0.2.0-preview`/`v0.3.0-preview` URLs; no
historical files have been replaced.

Verify every downloaded WAV against the published manifest's SHA-256 and
byte count before use.
