# Original procedural sound recordings and their use

**CC0 1.0 Universal public-domain dedication.** Leminno places the original
procedurally synthesized PCM audio generated by
[`tools/generate_original_sounds.py`](tools/generate_original_sounds.py)
under the Creative Commons Zero 1.0 Universal dedication:
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

The generator creates the audio mathematically from deterministic noise,
oscillators, resonant pulses and event envelopes. These files do **not** contain
third-party recordings, identifiable voices, music, or field-recorded animal
calls. The environmental names are sound-design impressions: for example,
an item labeled *Owl* is an artificial double-note hoot, not a recording
of a living owl.

The existing **third-party CC0 recordings** have different original creators
listed in [`manifest.json`](manifest.json) (published optional assets) and
[`bundled-cc0-recordings.json`](bundled-cc0-recordings.json) (already
included in the Android app). We do not claim to have made those recordings.

The Leminno name, logo, applications and branding are not covered by this
audio dedication. Technical audio checks are not listening approval.
